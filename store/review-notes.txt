turtl is an on-call assistant for people who run a live app without an operations team. It watches the owner's app, opens a case when a check fails or a customer reports a problem, explains the likely cause, and proposes fixes. Nothing changes in the owner's app until the owner approves it.

SIGNING IN
Use the review account from this submission: on the first screen tap Log in and enter its email and password. The first screen also offers Sign in with Apple, GitHub, and Sign up, which emails a link and a code to the address given; the new account opens once the code is entered (tap the link on the iPhone, or paste the code). The review account is already confirmed.

THE REVIEW ACCOUNT
A new account starts empty: it can connect a GitHub repository and a live app, or try a practice app that turtl runs for it. The review account has one project, "Practice booking app", connected to a small booking app we run only for practice and review, with a case already open.
1. If turtl asks about AI services, tap Allow or Not now. The case works either way.
2. On Projects tap Practice booking app, then Fix, then the issue "Booking is failing" (or swipe up).
3. Read Overview, Logs, and Code. The practice app has no repository, so Code says so.
4. Tap "Look at potential fixes", then Approve on "Turn off recommendations", then Approve on the sheet. turtl turns recommendations off in the practice app and runs its booking check again to confirm bookings work.
5. To repeat: on the project page tap Break it, choose what goes wrong, and tap Break it. A new issue opens under Fix within a minute or two; approve its fix as in step 4. This works up to 10 times a day.
6. On the project page, tap the gear for Settings: who approves fixes, AI services, Privacy policy, Terms, Support, and Account.
This only ever touches the review account's own practice app.

THIRD-PARTY AI (5.1.2)
turtl sends parts of the owner's source code, case details, messages, and voice recordings to third-party AI services. After sign-in, before anything is sent, a screen titled "Before turtl uses AI" names each service (OpenAI, Supermemory, and Capy), says what each receives, links to its policy, and asks with Allow and Not now. The backend refuses every call to a service the owner has not allowed. After Not now, turtl still watches the app and explains problems with its own rules. It can be taken back in Settings, under AI services.

MICROPHONE (2.5.14)
Used only after the user taps Talk on a case, while the app shows it is listening, for at most 30 seconds. The recording is sent to OpenAI to be turned into text and is not stored. Typing does the same job.

NOTIFICATIONS (4.5.4)
Optional; asked only after the user taps Turn on notifications. Sent only when a case needs a decision, a fix the owner allowed ran, or the app stops or starts answering turtl's checks. Never promotional.

ACCOUNT DELETION (5.1.1(v))
On Projects, tap the profile picture at the top right, then Delete account, Continue, and Yes, delete my account. This deletes the account and everything turtl holds for it, including the copies at our search provider and turtl's access to GitHub, and revokes the Apple token of a Sign in with Apple account.
Please try it on an account you create with Sign up, with an address you can read, not the review account: deleting that would end the review.

PURCHASES (3.1)
turtl is free. It has no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or advertising, and no links to buy anything. Code changes are written by Capy, a separate coding service the owner connects with their own key in Settings.

OTHER
- Code changes arrive as a pull request on GitHub, which the owner reviews and merges. The app does not download or run code.
- Sign in with GitHub and connecting repositories open GitHub's own pages in a browser sheet.
- People who use turtl cannot message each other, and nothing they write is shown to other users.
- The app needs a network connection. Our backend is running for the review.
